Semchaek Privacy Policy

Sangaji Studio ("we") makes the budget app Semchaek. This policy explains how personal information is handled, in line with Korea's Personal Information Protection Act. It is an English translation of our Korean policy. Effective date: October 23, 2026 (English version added November 6, 2026).

At a glance

  • Semchaek has no sign-up or login, and no ads or analytics tools.
  • What you write in your budget (amounts, descriptions, memos, books, categories, tags, payment methods and so on) is stored only on your phone. Semchaek has no server of ours, and we never receive your budget.
  • We receive personal information only when you email us yourself, including an email with an error log attached.
  • Semchaek sends only two things off your phone: the currency code and date when it looks up an exchange rate (section 2), and, only if you turn it on, a backup file to your own Google Drive (section 7). We receive neither.

1. What we process and why

  • If you email us: your email address, your message, and any files you attach (such as an error log). Purpose: to reply and solve the problem.
  • We do not collect or receive any other personal information.
  • Where the law asks for a legal basis, we rely on your request and our legitimate interest in answering you.

2. What leaves your phone: exchange rates

  • When you save an entry in a foreign currency, convert foreign-currency entries from an imported file, or when rates refresh once a day, Semchaek sends only a currency code and date to the exchange rate service Frankfurter (api.frankfurter.dev). Example: "USD, 2026-10-20".
  • Amounts, descriptions, memos and other budget content are not sent. The request goes straight from your phone to Frankfurter and does not pass through any server of ours.
  • As with any internet request, Frankfurter can see your phone's IP address. That is handled under Frankfurter's own policy, and we do not receive it. Semchaek stores only the rates it gets back on your phone.
  • If you use only your book's base currency and no other currency is added, no rate lookup happens. You can also type rates in yourself.

3. What you send yourself

  • Full backups, Excel exports and error logs go only to the place you pick in the share sheet (email, messenger, cloud and so on). Semchaek never sends them on its own.
  • An error log contains only the app version, Android version, device model, time, error type and code location. It contains no budget content such as amounts, descriptions, memos, names of books, categories or tags, or file names. Only the latest 20 logs are kept on your phone.
  • Paid themes: if you buy a theme, Google Play handles the payment, and Google handles your payment method and details (under Google's privacy policy and payment terms). Semchaek keeps only the list of bought themes on your phone, and when the app opens it asks Google Play what this account has bought. We can see sales records in the Google Play Console (order number, product, amount, country and so on), but we do not receive your name, email or payment method, and Semchaek does not send purchase records to us. Budget content is never sent, whether you buy or not.

4. How long we keep it, and deletion

  • We delete emails and attached files as soon as we have replied and solved the problem, and within one year of receiving them at the latest. If a law requires us to keep them longer, we keep them only for that period.
  • How we delete: we remove them fully from the mailbox and trash. We do not print them.
  • The budget on your phone stays until you delete it. Emptying the trash in Semchaek or deleting the app removes it from your phone. Backup files you saved separately and phone backups remain.

5. Sharing with others

  • We do not give your personal information to anyone else, and we do not sell it or share it for advertising.

6. Service providers and transfers abroad

  • We receive support email through Google's Gmail, so emails are stored on servers of Google LLC (in the United States and other countries).
  • Recipient: Google LLC (contact: https://support.google.com/policies)
  • Where, when and how: the countries where Google has servers, sent over the internet when you email us
  • What, why and how long: the items in section 1, to store email, for the period in section 4
  • How to refuse: if you do not email us, nothing is transferred. In that case we cannot reply to you.

7. Backups (phone backup, Google Drive backup)

  • If Google backup is on for your phone, Android backs up Semchaek's budget file and settings to your Google account storage. This is between you and Google, and we cannot see it.
  • To turn it off, go to Google › Backup in your phone's settings (menu names differ a little by phone).
  • Google Drive backup (optional): if you connect a Google account on Semchaek's backup screen, Semchaek uploads a full backup file (your whole budget and settings) to a "Semchaek Backup" folder in your own Google Drive. It uploads when you tap "Back up now" and, if you turn on automatic backup, on the schedule you set. The file goes straight from your phone to Google, not through any server of ours, and we cannot see it.
  • Semchaek asks only for the drive.file permission, which covers only files Semchaek made. It cannot see or change your other Drive files. The email address of the connected account is stored only on your phone, to show which account is connected.
  • To make backups easy to tell apart in the list, each backup file also records the entry count, app version, the model of the phone that uploaded it, and a random string that identifies that phone.
  • Semchaek keeps only the latest 5 backups and deletes older ones first. You can disconnect with "Disconnect" on the backup screen, and choose whether to keep or delete the backup files in Drive. Storing and handling files in Drive is between you and Google, under Google's privacy policy.

8. App permissions

  • Internet (required): for exchange rates and, if turned on, Google Drive backup.
  • Notifications (optional): backup reminders and recurring entry reminders. You can use Semchaek without allowing them.
  • Biometrics (optional): used only when you turn on app lock. Your phone handles biometric data such as fingerprints, and Semchaek does not receive it.
  • Vibration (required): a short vibration when you tap buttons. You can turn it off in Semchaek's settings.
  • Google Play billing (required): used only to buy paid themes and restore bought themes (section 3).
  • Semchaek does not use location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, SMS or phone permissions.

9. Cookies and similar tools

  • Semchaek and this policy page do not use cookies or analytics tools. This page is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may keep access logs.

10. Your rights

  • You (or your legal guardian, if you are under 14) can ask at any time to see, correct, delete or stop the processing of personal information we hold about you, such as your emails. Email us at the address below, and we will act without delay and tell you the result. This applies wherever you live, and you may have further rights under the laws of your country.
  • You can view, edit and delete the budget on your phone yourself in Semchaek.

11. How we protect information

  • We keep what we receive to a minimum (support email only). The mail account is protected by a password and two-step verification, and only we can see it.
  • Semchaek keeps your budget on your phone only, and offers app lock (fingerprint or phone lock). Files from other apps are checked for size and format before opening.

12. Children

  • Semchaek is not intended for children under 14, or under the minimum age set by the law of your country if that is higher, and we do not knowingly collect their personal information.
